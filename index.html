What Montazher does

We combine an editor's judgement with our own editing software that analyses speech, pauses and the picture in your footage.

CLEAN EDIT

Talking-head videos

We remove long pauses, breaths, filler words and failed takes, keep the natural rhythm of your speech and add captions.

SHORTS

Lessons into short clips

Long screen-recorded lessons are cut into self-contained vertical clips, each with one clear idea.

MOTION

Graphics, music and sound

Motion design that follows the meaning of what you say, licensed music and sound effects.

Delivered to your TikTok drafts

Instead of sending large files through messengers, Montazher can upload a finished video to your own TikTok account as a draft. You sign in with TikTok, choose a video and press one button. The video appears in your TikTok inbox, where you add a caption, edit and post it yourself. Montazher never posts on your behalf.
