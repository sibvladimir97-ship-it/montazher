How delivery to TikTok works

Montazher uses the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API (upload to drafts).

Open the Montazher app

The Montazher desktop app shows the list of your finished videos.

Continue with TikTok

You sign in on TikTok's own authorization page and approve the requested permissions. Montazher never sees your TikTok password.

Choose a video and send it to drafts

Montazher shows which TikTok account is connected (your display name and avatar). You press Send to TikTok drafts, and the video file is uploaded directly from your computer to TikTok.

Finish and post in TikTok

TikTok sends you an inbox notification. You open the draft in the TikTok app, edit it, add a caption and decide whether and when to post it.

Permissions we request

PermissionWhy Montazher needs it
user.info.basicTo show your display name and avatar, so you can see which TikTok account will receive the video before sending it.
video.uploadTo upload the finished video you selected to your TikTok drafts (inbox). Nothing is published automatically.

What Montazher does not do

  • It does not publish or schedule posts on your behalf.
  • It does not read your existing videos, followers, messages or analytics.
  • It does not upload anything you did not choose.

Disconnecting

Press Disconnect in the Montazher app to revoke access. You can also remove Montazher at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
