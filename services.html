Services

What Montazher edits and how each kind of video is handled.

01

Talking-head editing

Selfie and to-camera videos. We assemble the best takes, cut pauses, breaths and filler sounds at clean audio boundaries, and keep sound and picture in sync.

02

Captions

Word-accurate captions checked against what was actually said, styled for vertical video and safe from interface overlap.

03

Motion graphics

Titles, accents and illustrations that support the meaning of the speech, plus a hook in the first seconds.

04

Music and sound design

Licensed music beds and sound effects mixed under your voice.

05

Lessons into Shorts

Screen-recorded lessons cut into a series of vertical clips with on-screen actions preserved, so every step is easy to follow.

06

Promo videos

Short advertising videos built from a script, voice-over and real footage.

How we work with you

You send the footage

Raw recordings, a script if you have one, and any wishes about style.

We edit

An editor prepares the cut with the help of Montazher's software and checks every cut by ear.

You review

You watch the draft and send corrections; we apply them.

We deliver

The finished video is delivered as a file or straight to your TikTok drafts — see How it works.
