Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Montazher ("we", "us") collects when you use our video editing services and the Montazher app, including its TikTok integration, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Footage and materials you send us — recordings, scripts and other files you provide for editing.
  • Contact information — your name, email address or messenger contact that you use to communicate with us.
  • Information from TikTok, when you connect your account — your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (permission user.info.basic), and the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok so that the app can upload videos you choose to your drafts (permission video.upload) and post videos you choose to your profile (permission video.publish).

We do not collect your TikTok password. We do not access your existing TikTok videos, followers, messages or analytics.

2. How we use information

  • To edit your videos and deliver the finished result to you.
  • To show which TikTok account is connected, to post the videos you select with the settings you choose, and to upload videos you select to your TikTok drafts.
  • To communicate with you about your projects.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except TikTok itself when you choose to post or upload a video, and where required by law.

3. Where information is stored

TikTok tokens are stored only on the computer where the Montazher app runs, in a file readable only by its user account, and are used only to call TikTok's official API. Video files are uploaded directly from that computer to TikTok. Your footage is stored on our working computers for as long as needed for your project.

4. Retention and deletion

  • When you press Disconnect in the Montazher app, the TikTok token is revoked with TikTok and deleted from the computer.
  • You can also revoke access in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • You can ask us to delete your footage and contact information at any time by writing to sir.vovchik.97@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.

5. Security

We use TikTok's OAuth authorization with PKCE, keep TikTok credentials out of shared or public locations and transfer data to TikTok only over HTTPS.

6. Children

Montazher is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: sir.vovchik.97@gmail.com.
